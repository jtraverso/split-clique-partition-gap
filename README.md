# Nearly Quadratic Additive Gaps for Edge-Clique Partitions of Split Graphs

Juan Pablo Traverso Gianini · Preprint 1.0

For every sufficiently large order, the construction gives a split graph with a nearly quadratic gap between the integral and fractional optima for exact edge-clique partition, and vertex connectivity at least one fifth of its order. The stronger endpoint uses fractional pieces of order at most four. The paper derives the corresponding mixed-packing consequence using the completion identity.

[Preprint on alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.edge-clique-partition-split-graphs) · [Published sources and reproducibility audit](https://github.com/jtraverso/split-clique-partition-gap/tree/45a332df5197146f0fb576fb5fdef77e4303a785)

`SplitGapChallenge.lean` states the claims with a Mathlib-only import. `SplitGapSolution.lean` supplies the proofs from the substantive development in this source tree. The proof modules come from the published freeze. The submission adds only the comparison interface, metadata, licence and workflow.
