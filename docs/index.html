Clique partition gaps

Three papers on split graphs, from nearly quadratic gaps to exact recovery after random deletions.

Preprints · English and Spanish editions

  1. I

    Nearly Quadratic Additive Gaps for Edge-Clique Partitions of Split Graphs

    Constructs split graphs with a nearly quadratic gap between integral and fractional edge-clique partition numbers. Assigning core pairs to hosts transfers the gap from a residual packing problem.

  2. II

    Stability of the Clique Partition Gap in Split Graphs

    Bounds the change in the gap when required pairs lose their assignments. A single crossing-edge deletion attains the sharp factor of two. Protected assignments can also accommodate quadratically many deletions.

  3. III

    Recovering Host-Triangle Decompositions after Random Deletions in Split Graphs

    Recovers a balanced support after independent crossing-edge deletions. A single assignment preserves the nearly quadratic gap exactly with high probability and controls prescribed statistics on two scales.
