# Recovering Host-Triangle Decompositions after Random Deletions in Split Graphs

Juan Pablo Traverso Gianini · Preprint 1.0 · November 24, 2026

Part III of the series on clique partition gaps in split graphs. Part I realizes a residual packing gap by assigning core pairs to hosts. Part II bounds the effect of missing assignments. Part III reconstructs a balanced triangle support after random deletions. One resulting assignment preserves the gap and controls statistics both globally and on triangles with bases in the original core.
